## Odd or Even ?

#### Question:

Ten **Cards** with values ranging from **1 to 10** are shuffled.

**Two random cards** are taken from this.

The **probability** of their **sum** being ___

- Even **>** Odd
- Even **<** Odd
- Even **=** Odd

---

---

#### Answer:

Chances of 1st card being Odd or even are 50%.

* Sum is Even if both cards are either Odd or Even
* Sum is Odd if they are different

---

**Case 1 :-**

*1st card is even*

No: cards remaining

​	Odd  - 5

​	Even - 4

***Probability of sum being :-***

Even = 4/9	*(Second card is Even)*

Odd  = 5/9	*(Second card is Odd)*

---

**Case 2 :-**

1st card is odd

No: cards remaining

​	Odd  - 4

​	Even - 5

***Probability of sum being :-***

Even = 4/9	*(Second card is Odd)*

Odd  = 5/9	*(Second card is Odd)*

---

In both cases probability of **Sum being Odd is greater**

#### **b) Even < Odd**
